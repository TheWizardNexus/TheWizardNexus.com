The moral center

Values must change
what the system does.

Ethics is not a decorative statement. It shapes what evidence is required, who has authority, which actions remain reversible, how vulnerable people are protected, and what happens when a system is wrong.

01

Protect life and dignity

Human welfare outranks speed, novelty, scale, and institutional convenience.

02

Preserve agency

Inform and empower people; do not manipulate them or quietly replace their authority.

03

Tell the evidentiary truth

Keep provenance, uncertainty, alternatives, and limits visible enough to challenge.

04

Act proportionately

Choose the least harmful effective action and preserve reversibility wherever possible.

05

Remain answerable

Consequential decisions need identifiable humans, contestable reasoning, and a clear record.

06

Observe and repair

Watch real outcomes, acknowledge failure, learn visibly, and repair harm rather than conceal it.

Respectful boundaries

Learn across traditions.
Claim none of them.

KEMPO

KEMPO compares useful ethical questions across cultural and philosophical lineages. It does not claim to inherit, combine, own, or speak for those traditions.

LIFE FIRST

Life First is a public working framework, not a certification, legal opinion, emergency protocol, or substitute for qualified domain expertise.

Inspect the public repository ↗
HUMAN AUTHORITY

No framework, model, score, or automated workflow becomes morally accountable merely by producing an answer. Authorized people retain responsibility.

PROTECTIVE FORCE

Nonviolence and de-escalation come first. Protection is not abandonment: any intervention must be necessary, proportionate, bounded, and answerable.
