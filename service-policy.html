How it works

Bounded.
Evidence-led.

  1. 01
    Scope and authority

    Identify the policy purpose, governing sources, owners, affected people, and non-negotiable boundaries.

  2. 02
    Audit and gap map

    Compare the current state with requirements, real workflows, and known failure points.

  3. 03
    Draft and redline

    Build a concise policy architecture and resolve material wording choices transparently.

  4. 04
    Stakeholder review

    Test usability, inclusion, operational fit, and accountability with the responsible groups.

  5. 05
    Simulation

    Walk through representative cases and record where decisions or handoffs break down.

  6. 06
    Final pack

    Deliver the controlled policy, procedures, aids, change log, and review plan.
