The PreCrisis lifecycle

Readiness before response.

This lifecycle is an organizing practice, not a diagnosis or promise of prediction. It helps teams move from better conditions toward proportionate human support.

  1. 01
    Optimize

    Strengthen readiness, resilience, connection, and the conditions in which people operate.

  2. 02
    Detect

    Notice meaningful change while keeping context, uncertainty, and human dignity visible.

  3. 03
    Prevent

    Prepare humane, proportionate support before pressure becomes an emergency.

  4. 04
    Intervene

    Equip an informed, authorized person to decide and act at the responsible moment.

The ethical operating system

Each instrument answers a different question. Together they connect early awareness, practiced judgment, governed action, and accountable human choice.

KEMPO // practiced judgment

An AI martial art
for the mind.

PreCrisis helps people notice meaningful change. KEMPO trains and tests how an AI reasons when facts alone are not enough, across behavioral safety, ethics, cyber, crisis, and public safety.

  2. EEmpowerEquip people without replacing their agency or authority.
  3. MMonitorStay attentive to change, impact, and emerging risk.
  4. PPreventPrefer humane, proportionate action before harm escalates.
