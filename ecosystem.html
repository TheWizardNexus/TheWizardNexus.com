What You Can Explore in Astrolabe
  • Foundations
    What principles guide the work?

    Protect life and dignity, preserve human agency, and keep consequential decisions accountable to people. Life First provides a related ethical framework; Compass contributes moral-reasoning material, and KEMPO evaluates judgment through human scoring.

  • Platforms
    What supports the applications?

    Shared technical foundations. ARCANE OS provides an application environment, while the Arcane OS SDK supplies tools for building and packaging applications. Individual projects retain their own development stages.

  • Products
    Which tools address which needs?

    PreCrisis AI focuses on earlier recognition of risk and human-led intervention. Redress addresses legal-practice work, and Sentinel turns complex records into source-cited leads and reports. These are distinct applications, not interchangeable services.

  • Programs
    How is the work organized into initiatives?

    Programs connect a defined purpose, participants, and delivery activities. For example, the developing Warrior Spirit Companion is a Veteran-community PreCrisis initiative. A program’s status and organizational owner matter separately from the technology it uses.

  • Audiences
    Who is the work intended to serve?

    Clinicians, public institutions, military and Veteran communities, researchers, schools, technical teams, and mission-driven leaders. Different projects serve different groups; the map helps distinguish those intended relationships.

  • Funding
    What does funding mean in this map?

    The support and resources associated with sustaining initiatives. A mapped funding relationship does not, by itself, establish an award, available funding, or a committed partnership. Those details need confirmation from the specific source.

  • Native Hosts
    Where are systems designed to run?

    The environments that host the work, such as a browser, a local device, or an application environment. DBOPFS stores application data locally in the browser; ARCANE OS is an application environment in development. Hosting and deployment requirements remain project-specific.
